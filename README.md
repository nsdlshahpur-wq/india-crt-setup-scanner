# India CRT Setup Scanner

A Streamlit dashboard for the Monthly (1M) → Daily (1D) CRT scanner and historical backtest.

## Dashboard

The app includes the CRT Scanner, historical Backtest and an in-app CRT AI Assistant. The assistant can explain the strategy and interpret the latest scanner/backtest data available in the current app session.

## Strategy overview

1. **M1:** Previous completed month's High–Low liquidity range.
2. **M2:** Sweep exactly one side of M1 and close back inside the full M1 range.
3. **M3:** Use current-month completed Daily candles for CHoCH and entry-zone analysis.
4. After CHoCH, look for a retest in one of FVG, IFVG, OB or BOB, followed by a directional Daily confirmation close.
5. **Stop loss:** Actual M2 sweep-candle extreme. **Target:** Opposite M1 liquidity.
6. Only a fresh confirmation on the latest completed Daily candle is labelled BUY/SELL. Older untriggered opportunities are shown as missed/stale, not fresh entries.

These are explicit mechanical OHLC rules. They may differ from discretionary chart interpretations and do not guarantee profitable signals.

## Historical backtesting and reproducibility

- Backtest universe: current BSE EQ scrip master (Yahoo Finance `.BO` tickers where data is available).
- Default test request: 5 years of Daily OHLC, ₹100,000 starting capital, 1% equity risk per trade.
- Signals are generated by evaluating the unchanged scanner against an as-of prefix ending on each historical completed candle. No later candle is visible when a signal is created.
- Entry is the confirming Daily candle close. SL/target checks start on the following candle. If one candle touches both SL and target, the result is marked ambiguous and conservatively priced at SL.
- Position size is risk-budget based and capped by available capital; simultaneous exposure is capped to available equity and duplicate overlapping positions in the same symbol are skipped.
- Default estimated costs are 12 bps transaction charges and 5 bps slippage per side; both are editable. These are approximations, not a contract-note tax calculator.
- The first 80 Daily bars per symbol are warm-up, so the effective test window begins after that warm-up. Insufficient history and unavailable tickers are reported.
- Win rate excludes ambiguous and end-of-data open trades. Profit factor includes conservative ambiguous P&L. The equity curve and drawdown are based on realised exits; open trades are marked to the last available close in final P&L.

**Important:** The BSE universe is the current exchange master, not historical point-in-time constituents. Delisted stocks and past constituent changes are missing (survivorship bias). Yahoo Finance history can be incomplete, adjusted or unavailable. Results are research estimates, not guaranteed or executable performance.

## Central AI market intelligence

The single Dashboard AI Command Centre combines:
- Gemini explanations using the configured `GEMINI_API_KEY` and the current scanner/backtest session records.
- Local quantitative breadth and regime snapshot from downloaded OHLC: advances/declines, percentage above SMA20/SMA50, 20-day returns, RSI(14), volatility, and strongest/weakest symbols.
- Five-pair CRT setup summaries from the scanner records.
- Optional public Google News RSS headline mood when the user asks about news/sentiment. The first implementation uses a transparent positive/negative finance keyword baseline, not FinBERT; it reports this limitation explicitly.

The local regime label is rule-based, not an ML prediction. Breadth covers only symbols with sufficient valid downloaded history. Google News RSS coverage and headlines can be incomplete, duplicated or delayed. No paid news API key is required for this baseline.

FinBERT is not bundled by default: the commonly used ProsusAI model weights are licensed CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, which restricts commercial use. Review model and dataset terms before adding any pretrained weights.

## Market data and limitations

- Daily OHLC history is downloaded from Yahoo Finance through `yfinance`.
- Yahoo Finance is an unofficial data source. Prices may be delayed, incomplete, adjusted, throttled or unavailable.
- The displayed LTP is the latest downloaded Daily close, not a live tick quote.
- Verify every candidate on a broker or TradingView chart before making a trading decision.
- The scanner is read-only and does not place, modify or cancel orders.

- `.github/workflows/crt-bot.yml` schedules a scan every 30 minutes on weekdays during the approximate Indian cash-market session (UTC cron).

## Daily CRT Hybrid AI and full-market ensemble

- The **CRT AI Lab** is integrated in the `main` branch at `pages/3_CRT_AI_Lab.py`. It loads the public NIFTY50 1-minute history, rebuilds Daily OHLC and tests the existing Monthly-Daily CRT rules.
- The main scanner supports **All India stocks + indices** (NSE EQ + BSE EQ + indices with an explicit Yahoo Finance ticker mapping), as well as NSE-only and index-only scans.
- The main app's CRT Multi-AI ensemble uses three independent classifiers: Random Forest, Extra Trees and Histogram Gradient Boosting. Their probabilities are combined with equal-weight soft voting.
- The ensemble is trained from completed, historical CRT WIN/LOSS trades generated by the five-year All India stock + mapped index backtest. It then scores only fresh Stage-3 confirmed BUY/SELL setups found in the next full-market scan. Waiting, stale, ambiguous and non-entry stages are not assigned a success probability.
- Features are available at entry time: direction, stop/target distance, reward-to-risk, FVG/IFVG/OB/BOB, M1 range, sweep depth, CHoCH distance, zone width and entry month. Future exit fields are labels only and never features.
- Model validation is chronological (75% train / 25% unseen test). Individual model scores, ensemble accuracy, balanced accuracy and Brier score are shown. At least 45 decisive closed trades and 10 examples of each outcome are required; otherwise the app reports insufficient data.
- To activate ensemble scoring in a fresh app session: run **Backtest → Run 5-Year Full-Market Backtest**, wait for training, then run **CRT Scanner → All India stocks + indices → Scan market**. The trained model is held in the current Streamlit session; retrain after restarting the app.
- The Gemini-powered CRT AI Assistant remains a separate language assistant for explaining strategy and current scan/backtest data. Its API key is optional and must be stored in Streamlit Secrets, never in Git.
- All model probabilities are research estimates, not calibrated guarantees or order signals. Current exchange constituents and Yahoo Finance coverage have survivorship/data-quality limitations. Validate out-of-sample and in paper trading before relying on them.

## Experimental Quantum AI research engine

- The single Dashboard AI Command Centre can run the experimental quantum-kernel analysis when asked for “Quantum AI analysis”.
- It uses a four-qubit NumPy state-vector simulator on a conventional CPU; it does **not** connect to quantum hardware.
- The quantum kernel classifies Daily OHLC feature snapshots (trend gaps, 5/20-session momentum, RSI and realised volatility) as bullish / bearish / neutral. It also compares a chronological holdout score with a classical RBF-kernel baseline.
- Strongest / weakest symbols are ranked by observed 20-session return. Existing Daily CRT setup, Entry, SL and target fields are attached from the scanner when available. The five CRT MTF pair records are attached from the MTF screener when available.
- Important limitation: the quantum circuit itself is currently trained on Daily OHLC only. The five timeframe results are existing CRT screener context, not five separate quantum-trained models. Volume/liquidity is not yet a quantum model feature; no reliable order-book liquidity feed is connected.
- Kernel class scores are not calibrated probabilities. The output is not an automatic BUY/SELL instruction and does not place trades. Treat the holdout comparison as a small research diagnostic, not proof of predictive edge. Validate with larger walk-forward tests and paper trading before relying on it.
- To use it, run **Dashboard → Scan market** first, and run **Multi-Symbol Screener** if five-pair CRT context is required. Then ask the central AI: “पूरे मार्केट का Quantum AI analysis करो”.


## Multi-Asset CISD + FVG Scanner

- Page: `pages/6_XAUUSD_Gold_CISD_Scanner.py`; engine: `gold_cisd_scanner.py`.
- Yahoo Finance instrument selector includes Metals (XAUUSD, XAGUSD), Crypto (BTCUSD, ETHUSD, SOLUSD, BNBUSD, XRPUSD, ADAUSD, DOGEUSD, LTCUSD), Forex (EURUSD, GBPUSD, USDJPY, AUDUSD, USDCAD, USDCHF, NZDUSD, EURJPY, GBPJPY, EURGBP), and US30 via the `YM=F` E-mini Dow futures proxy.
- No stocks/equities are included in this scanner.
- Yahoo tickers are provider symbols, not necessarily the exact CFD/spot contract used by a broker. US30 is a futures proxy, not a broker US30 CFD. Yahoo intraday feeds may be delayed, limited, incomplete, or unavailable.
- Fetches latest available 1-minute quote (falls back to 5-minute) and up to 60 days of 5-minute history when Yahoo supports the selected symbol.
- Rule sequence: previous-session high/low sweep and reclaim → CISD body close → three-candle FVG → FVG retest/rejection → next-bar entry. Stop is beyond the sweep extreme; default RR 1:3.
- Backtest results are in instrument price units, not account-currency or broker lot-based P&L. Spread, commission, swap, contract size and execution differ by broker. No orders are placed.


## Separate CISD Liquidity Sweep Scanner

- New page: `pages/5_CISD_Liquidity_Scanner.py`.
- Engine: `cisd_scanner.py`; offline rules tests: `test_cisd_scanner.py`.
- Uses NIFTY 50 1-minute historical OHLC already provided by `nifty50_history.py`, resampled to NSE-aligned 5-minute candles.
- Mechanical sequence from the uploaded CISD lesson: previous-session liquidity sweep and reclaim → CISD line at the open of the first candle in the immediately preceding opposing-colour run → body close across CISD (wick-only does not count) → three-candle FVG → FVG retest/rejection → next-bar-open entry.
- Stop is beyond the sweep extreme. To honour the requested 1:3 RR while preserving the lesson's old-high/old-low liquidity objective, the opposite previous-session liquidity must be at least 3R away; otherwise the setup is skipped. The backtest target is fixed at 3R.
- Backtest reports index points, not option/futures rupee P&L. Costs are configurable estimates. The original CRT scanner and its rules are unchanged.
- Mechanical assumptions are explicit approximations where the short lesson does not define swing lookback, candle-run qualification, or retest confirmation. Validate against chart examples before live use.
